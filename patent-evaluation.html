04

Methodology and boundaries

Designed for transparent inspection, not legal conclusions.

Claim fidelity: checks whether configured source limitations remain present in the candidate text.
Unsupported assertions: flags configured technical features appearing in the candidate but not the source.
Citation verification: checks citation identifiers against the supplied evidence-ID set. It does not verify substantive support.
Claim dependency: flags explicitly configured references to unavailable or later claims.
Antecedent basis: flags a configured definite element when no indefinite introduction appears in the candidate.
Deterministic gate: schema-backed fixture inputs and explicit rule findings follow Cognisynth's principle that generated output is untrusted until independently checked.

Limitations: synthetic, hand-authored fixtures; string and pattern heuristics; no live LLM call; no external prior-art retrieval; no novelty, inventive-step, infringement or legal-sufficiency analysis. A 100% score means only that the configured synthetic failure labels were detected by these configured rules.
